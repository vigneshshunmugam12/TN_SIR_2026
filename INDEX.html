SIR Deletion Data Repository

Constituency-wise deletions from the SIR draft roll, matched with 2021 and 2026 election results and turnout for all 234 Tamil Nadu assembly constituencies. Click any column to sort, click any row for detail.

Reading the data. Deletions are concentrated in low-turnout, mostly urban seats. TVK won most of the heaviest-deletion seats, but that overlaps with the Chennai belt, so it is a correlation, not proof of cause. Whether removed voters leaned one way cannot be known without booth-level lists.

Sources and limits. 2021 and 2026 results, electors and turnout: Election Commission of India. Draft-roll deletions (deceased / shifted-absent / duplicate) and draft roll: Chief Electoral Officer, Tamil Nadu, via data.opencity.in. Published totals: 97,37,832 names removed; draft roll 5,43,76,755; about 74.07 lakh net removal at the 23 Feb final roll (DT Next); about 7.40 lakh first-time voters aged 18–19 reported statewide (CEO, via Onmanorama). Not available by constituency in the sources used: how many deleted names were later restored, and how many first-time voters were added. "Added after draft" is the 2026 poll roll minus the draft roll and mixes restored voters, new voters and later updates. Turnout = valid votes (excluding NOTA) ÷ registered electors. TVK did not contest in 2021. This page shows constituency totals only, not individual voters.
