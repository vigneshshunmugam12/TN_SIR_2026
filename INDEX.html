Logical & Statistical Conclusion: Correlation vs. Causation

Evaluating the hypothesis of voter roll deletions as a causal determinant of election outcomes.

1. Urban Tenancy Confounder

Over 68% of statewide exclusions were categorized as "Shifted/Absent", spiking in high-density IT/industrial corridors (Chennai, Coimbatore, Chengalpattu). These same urban zones house young, mobile demographics where alternative political mobilization naturally concentrates. Both variables correlate with urbanization, not with each other.

2. Margin Safety Buffers

In top-deletion constituencies (e.g., Madavaram margin: 57,071; Pallavaram margin: 37,783; Shozhinganallur margin: 35,405), the actual winning margins exceed realistic field verification clerical error bounds (~3,000 to 8,000 votes) by orders of magnitude.

3. Non-Partisan Scrubbing

Unlike West Bengal's algorithmic 2002 baseline linkage that generated severe community disparities and appellate tribunal intervention, Tamil Nadu's SIR was a non-partisan administrative clean-up targeting deceased and duplicate electors across all households equally.
